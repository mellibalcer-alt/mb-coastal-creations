meet the maker

Hi, I'm Melli.

I've always loved making things — painting, crafting, decorating, designing, and coming up with something new just because I can.

MB Coastal Creations grew from creating things for myself, friends, and family into a little collection I can share with other people. I love soft blues, coastal and nautical details, thoughtful gifts, and pieces that feel personal rather than mass-produced.

You might find jewelry one week, candles or soaps the next, then ornaments, seasonal décor, or a completely random new idea. Everything is made in small batches or individually, so the shop can keep changing with me.

— Melli ♡
